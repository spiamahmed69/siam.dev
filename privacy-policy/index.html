Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026.

What this website collects

SIAM.DEV does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no newsletter sign-ups, no analytics trackers and no advertising on this site. The theme preference you choose (dark or light) is stored only in your own browser, and you can clear it at any time by clearing your browser data.

Contact form

The contact form does not send or store anything on a server. When you submit it, your own email app opens with a message addressed to spsiamahmed1111@gmail.com. If you choose to send that email, it is handled by your email provider under its own privacy policy.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical information (such as your IP address and the pages you visit) in its server logs for security and reliability. SIAM.DEV does not receive or use that information.

External links

This site links to external platforms — GitHub, YouTube, Facebook and the live demos of my projects. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and SIAM.DEV is not responsible for their content or practices.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above will change too. Questions about this policy can be sent to spsiamahmed1111@gmail.com.
