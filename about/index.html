About Me

About Me

A Passion for Technology and Web Development.

I’m SP Siam Ahmed, a web developer from Bangladesh with a strong interest in web technologies, digital products and user-friendly design.

I enjoy creating websites that combine visual quality with practical functionality. My interests include frontend development, backend systems, responsive web design, database integration and website optimization.

I continuously explore new technologies, experiment with ideas and improve my development skills through practical projects. Most of what I know comes from building real things — learning platforms, calculators, calendars, bookstores and exam systems — and then fixing what breaks.

My Mission

To build useful, accessible and well-designed digital experiences while continuing to grow as a developer.

I care about websites that load fast, work on small phones as well as large screens, and are usable by everyone — including people who navigate with a keyboard or a screen reader.
